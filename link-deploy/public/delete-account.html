Account & Data Deletion

Instructions on how to permanently delete your Hakkiri account, voice recordings, and associated personal data.

Method 1: Delete Directly Inside the Hakkiri App (Instant)

You can permanently delete your account directly inside the iOS or Android mobile app at any time:

  1. Open the Hakkiri mobile app and ensure you are logged in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom of the Profile screen.
  4. Tap the red Delete Account button (located below Log out).
  5. Confirm the deletion in the confirmation dialog.

Permanent Once confirmed, your user account, login credentials, practice recordings, transcripts, and drill scores are permanently erased from our database immediately.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email Support

If you have already uninstalled the app or prefer our privacy team to remove your account, you can submit an email deletion request:

  • Send an email from your registered account email address to hakkiriadmin@gmail.com.
  • Include the subject line: Request Account & Data Deletion.
  • Include your registered Full Name and Email Address.

Our security team will verify the request and permanently delete your account and all associated records within 48 hours.

Request Deletion: hakkiriadmin@gmail.com

What Data is Deleted?

When an account is deleted, the following data is permanently purged from our servers:

  • Account Profile: Full name, email address, password hash, and social login markers.
  • Practice Sessions: All verbal drill recordings, transcripts, clarity scores, filler word statistics, and feedback logs.
  • Personalized Plan: Career milestones, speaking confidence levels, and recommended tracks.
  • Reflections & Notes: All notes to future self, session takeaways, and journal entries.
  • Practice Streaks & History: Daily streak counts, freezes, and completed challenge milestones.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase device notification tokens are immediately unlinked and removed.

We do not retain backup copies of your voice recordings or profile once deletion is confirmed.
