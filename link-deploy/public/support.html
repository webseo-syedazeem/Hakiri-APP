Frequently Asked Questions

How does Hakkiri train impromptu communication?
Hakkiri provides fast, targeted voice drills (Think Fast, Storytelling Gym, Debate Me, Cut It in Half) that present surprise questions or challenging pushbacks. You have a few seconds to organize your thoughts, record your response, and receive instant AI analysis on clarity, conciseness, pacing, and filler words.
Are my voice recordings private and secure?
Yes. Your audio recordings are strictly confidential. The microphone is only active while you are holding or tapping the record button. Recordings are never broadcast publicly and are never used to train public foundational AI models.
How does the AI Voice Coach work?
You can select a specialized voice coach personality (e.g. Socratic, Direct Executive, Empathetic). During drills like Debate Me, the coach listens to your response and provides live, spoken audio pushback so you learn to think under pressure.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are processed through the official app store. On iOS, open device Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Hakkiri. On Android, open the Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & Subscriptions. You can cancel anytime.
How do I permanently delete my account and data?
Open Hakkiri → go to the Profile tab → scroll to the bottom → tap Delete Account. Confirming will erase your account and all voice recordings immediately. You can also view details at /delete-account.
