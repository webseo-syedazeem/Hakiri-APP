Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Hakkiri application (hereby referred to as the “Application”) for mobile devices, created by Hakkiri (hereby referred to as the “Service Provider”) as a professional speech and communication training service. This service is intended for use “AS IS.”

Private & Confidential by Design

Hakkiri is engineered for executives, professionals, and communicators who value strict privacy. Your spoken responses, voice practice recordings, and personalized coaching evaluations are strictly confidential and are never used for public AI training or sold to third parties.

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects limited information to deliver AI-powered speech analysis and structured exercises:

  • Account Information: Your full name, email address, and authentication credentials when you create an account or sign in with Apple/Google.
  • Voice and Speech Recordings: Spoken audio captured strictly while you are actively holding or tapping the record button during a guided drill (e.g. Think Fast, Storytelling Gym, Debate Me). The microphone is never accessed in the background.
  • Practice Metrics & Performance: Speech metrics including pace (words per minute), filler word frequency, clarity, conciseness, milestone completions, and practice streaks.
  • Device & Usage Information: Operating system, app version, IP address, and interaction diagnostics to ensure reliable speech recognition and low-latency audio processing.

Microphone Permission

The Application requires microphone access exclusively to record your verbal answers during active exercises. Audio recording begins only when you trigger a drill and stops automatically when the exercise ends. You can revoke microphone permissions at any time in your device Settings.

Payments and Subscription Data

Hakkiri offers premium membership plans for personalized curriculum access. Subscriptions are billed through official platform stores: Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit) on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android. The Application never collects or stores your credit card number, CVV, or bank account details on its servers.

Third-Party Services

To provide high-quality speech analysis, push notifications, and app stability, the Application integrates trusted industry infrastructure:

  • Google Play Services (authentication and core Android APIs)
  • Apple StoreKit & Google Play Billing (secure subscription management)
  • Firebase Crashlytics (anonymized crash diagnostics)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (daily practice reminders and streak alerts)

Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your user profile and practice sessions are retained while your account remains active. You can permanently delete your account, recordings, transcripts, and personal data at any time directly inside the Application (via Profile → Delete Account) or by submitting a deletion request at /delete-account.

Children’s Privacy

The Application is designed for working professionals and learners and is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/HTTPS in transit and secure database storage at rest) to safeguard your profile and practice data against unauthorized access.
