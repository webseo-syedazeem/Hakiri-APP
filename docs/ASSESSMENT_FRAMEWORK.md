# Hakkiri — Assessment Framework & Personalization Engine

## 1. Overview
The onboarding self-assessment consists of 6 targeted questions designed to map the user's biological vocal structure, industry vernacular, psychological friction points, and career goals. 

Based on their answers, the engine generates:
1. **Industry Prompt Routing**: Tailoring drills to their field (e.g., Tech gets architecture refactoring and sprint post-mortems; Sales gets deal objections; Healthcare gets patient communication).
2. **Personalized 4-Week AI Roadmap**: Curating specific daily drills that focus on their primary roadblock.
3. **Calibrated Baseline Difficulty**: Starting level recommendations (Beginner, Intermediary, or Professional).

---

## 2. Assessment Questions & Options

### Question 1: Voice Profile & Biological Vocal Alignment
*Which voice profile best aligns with your biological vocal structure and personalized training goals?*
- **Male**: Focused on deep chest resonance and steady projection.
- **Female**: Focused on pitch clarity and head-to-chest tonal transitions.
- **Non-Binary / Vocal Alignment**: Focused on customizable pitch matching and vocal safety.

### Question 2: Career Stage
*Where are you currently at in your professional journey?*
- **Student / Early Career**: Preparing for interviews, behavioral questions, and entry-level team entries.
- **Mid-Level Professional**: Leading team meetings, cross-functional projects, performance reviews, and cross-team alignment.
- **Senior Executive / Founder**: Delivering board updates, fundraising pitches, media keynotes, and crisis communications.
- **General Self-Learner**: Focused on personal development, social presence, and conversational wit.

### Question 3: Career Type & Industry Track
*Which field best describes your primary work environment?*
- **Corporate & Finance**: Structured meetings, data reviews, quarterly performance forecasts, and formal presentations.
- **Tech, Engineering & Product**: Translating complex architecture into simple terms, managing technical debt, sprint retro alignment.
- **Sales, Marketing & Content Creator**: High-energy persuasion, objection handling, negotiation, and audience engagement.
- **Healthcare & Education**: Clear, patient, empathetic, highly structured, and de-escalation communication.

### Question 4: Verbal Speaking Confidence
*How do you honestly feel when you have to speak or present under pressure?*
- **Anxious & Shaky**: Throat tightens, heart races, speaking too fast, cognitive freeze.
- **Moderately Confident**: Able to deliver the core message, but prone to filler words ("um", "like", "you know") and trailing off.
- **Highly Confident**: Comfortable controlling the room, looking to master advanced nuances like power pauses, uptalk elimination, and rhetorical agility.

### Question 5: Primary Roadblock to Solve
*What is the biggest roadblock holding your voice back right now?*
- **Fast Talking & Mumbling**: Losing breath control, running out of breath, poor consonant articulation.
- **Excessive Filler Words**: Crutch words ("um", "uh", "sort of", "basically", "does that make sense?").
- **Monotone / Flat Delivery**: Lack of pitch and decibel dynamics, sounding unengaged or robotic.
- **Accent Barriers / Pronunciation**: Struggling with phoneme clarity or feeling misunderstood in global/virtual meetings. *(Note: Accents are never penalized; intelligibility and articulation are coached).*

### Question 6: Primary Goal
*If you could fix one thing by using this app daily, what would it be?*
- **Executive Presence**: Speak with authority, eliminate uptalk, project calm competence.
- **Reduce Anxiety & Freeze**: Overcome panic when put on the spot in meetings.
- **Sharp On-the-Fly Arguments**: Learn to debate, handle objections, and reframe opposing views.
- **Diction & Vocal Stamina**: Improve daily vocal projection, resonance, and articulation.

## 3. Personalization & AI Curriculum Logic

| Roadblock / Goal | Recommended Primary MVP Drills | Target Success Metrics |
|---|---|---|
| **Freeze Under Pressure / Anxiety** | `Think Fast`, `Connect the Dots` | Latency reduction, intentional silence framing |
| **Rambling & Waffling** | `Cut It In Half`, `1 Minute Expert` | Rate compression (50%+), Time to Value (BLUF) |
| **Monotone / Lack of Engagement** | `Storytelling Gym`, `Debate Me` | Pitch (Hz) and Loudness (dB) variance |
| **Jargon & Stakeholder Misalignment** | `Explain It Simply` | Persona calibration, linguistic fit |
| **Lack of Authority / Uptalk** | `Debate Me`, `Think Fast` | Power pause reward, uptalk penalization |
