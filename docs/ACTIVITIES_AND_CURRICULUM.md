# Hakkiri — Activities Catalog & Curriculum Matrix

## 1. Catalog Overview

Hakkiri features 12 core communication drills, with **7 prioritized MVP activities**:

| Activity Name | Status | Cognitive Mechanism | Time Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Think Fast\*** | **MVP** | Unscripted spontaneous reasoning under time pressure | 3s wait, 60s speech |
| **Cut It In Half\*** | **MVP** | Rapid synthesis, information compression, BLUF | 45s read, 30–60s speech |
| **1 Minute Expert\*** | **MVP** | Thematic chunking & spontaneous executive thesis | 60s speech |
| **Explain It Simply\*** | **MVP** | Persona calibration, translation of complexity | 60s speech |
| **Storytelling Gym\*** | **MVP** | 3-part corporate narrative arc, strategic takeaways | 60s speech |
| **Connect the Dots\*** | **MVP** | Lateral associative bridge between 2 random topics | 5s wait, 60s speech |
| **Debate Me\*** | **MVP** | Composure under pushback, counter-framing | 60s speech |
| *Build Your Point* | v2 | Argument structuring (PREP framework: Point, Reason, Example, Point) | 90s |
| *Vocabulary Upgrade* | v2 | Precision lexicon swap (replacing generic verbs with executive terms) | 60s |
| *Listen and Respond* | v2 | Active listening, playback confirmation, clarifying questions | 60s |
| *Conversation Flow* | v2 | Small talk to substantive transition, social bridging | 120s |
| *Speak Up* | v2 | Vocal projection, interjection timing, room commanding | 45s |

## 2. MVP Activities Detail & Prompt Banks

### 1. Think Fast\*
- **Problem**: Early graduates and professionals freeze or ramble when asked off-the-cuff questions.
- **Workflow**: A random prompt flashes on screen. The user gets a 3-second countdown. The app records for 60 seconds.
- **Level Matrix**:
  - **Beginner**: Objective: Fill the silence and survive the clock.
    - *Prompt 1*: Should companies completely ban video calls on Fridays? Why or why not?
    - *Prompt 2*: If you could instantly become an expert in any one skill tomorrow, what would it be and why?
    - *Prompt 3*: Is it better to work for a massive multinational corporation or a 10-person startup at the beginning of your career?
    - *Prompt 4*: What is one everyday habit or tool that you believe drastically improves your productivity?
  - **Intermediary**: Objective: Answer using a structured framework with controlled panic.
    - *Prompt 1*: Your team has missed a major project deadline due to a miscommunication. How do you explain this to your client without blaming your team?
    - *Prompt 2*: A colleague strongly disagrees with your project proposal in a meeting. How do you respond to them on the spot?
    - *Prompt 3*: If a manager tells you, 'We don't have the budget for your professional development request this year,' how do you negotiate?
    - *Prompt 4*: How would you explain the concept of 'Return on Investment' (ROI) to a non-technical stakeholder?
  - **Professional**: Objective: Deliver a memorable executive narrative with thematic bucketing.
    - *Prompt 1*: Our company’s core software platform just went offline globally, and clients are panicking on social media. Give an immediate 60-second update to the board of directors.
    - *Prompt 2*: Artificial intelligence is rapidly commoditizing our core service offering. What is our company’s new competitive advantage?
    - *Prompt 3*: In business, is it more dangerous to move too slowly with perfect data, or move too quickly with flawed data?
    - *Prompt 4*: Reframe this statement: 'Our competitor's product is significantly cheaper than ours, so we are losing market share.' Speak to the sales team to pivot their strategy.

### 2. Cut It In Half\*
- **Problem**: Over-explaining, hiding requests under fluff, burdening listeners with cognitive load.
- **Workflow**: A longer passage is presented. The user gets time to read it, then **60 seconds to summarise** it out loud — keep the key points, cut the fluff (≥50% compression).
- **Level Matrix**:
  - **Beginner**: Start speaking within 3s, state the primary point, steady pace.
    - *Prompt (Slack Policy)*: "Hey team, I just wanted to jump on here and let everyone know that we’ve been looking at how we manage our internal communications lately. It turns out that a lot of people are feeling pretty overwhelmed by the sheer volume of Slack messages flying around every day, especially after hours. Because of that, leadership has decided that starting next Monday, we are going to implement a new policy where no one is expected to reply to any Slack messages after 6:00 PM. We really want everyone to get some proper downtime so we can keep our productivity high during normal working hours."
  - **Intermediary**: Hit the main point within the middle, achieve 50% compression rate, controlled vocabulary.
    - *Prompt (CAC & Margin Gap)*: "As we review our financial performance metrics for the third quarter of this fiscal year, the data indicates a highly concerning downward trend. Specifically, our customer acquisition costs have escalated by a significant 22% compared to the previous quarter, which is largely driven by increased ad bidding competition on our primary paid social media channels. Concurrently, our overall conversion rates have flattened out entirely. To address this widening margin gap, the marketing department needs to immediately pause all active top-of-funnel ad campaigns by Friday afternoon so we can reallocate our remaining budget into our high-performing email retention strategies."
  - **Professional**: Paraphrase corporate jargon, hit the main takeaway within first 5 seconds, cut ≥60% of words with punchy delivery.
    - *Prompt (SaaS Model Transition)*: "In light of shifting macroeconomic tailwinds and aggressive market penetration by low-cost, decentralized competitors, our current enterprise SaaS monetization framework is experiencing unprecedented margin erosion. While our legacy client footprint remains relatively insulated due to multi-year contractual lock-ins, our net new logo acquisition velocity has decelerated by a critical margin. Consequently, cross-functional stakeholders must urgently initiate a comprehensive pricing restructuring paradigm. We need to transition from our traditional seat-based licensing model to a consumption-based utility model before the upcoming annual shareholder summit to safeguard our projected valuation targets."

### 3. 1 Minute Expert\*
- **Problem**: Subject matter experts freeze, ramble, or fail to summarize complex domains under spontaneous requests.
- **Workflow**: User selects or is assigned a topic. 60s speech with bottom-line-up-front (BLUF) and conclusion.
- **Level Matrix**:
  - **Beginner**: Speak for 60s without running out of ideas.
    - *Prompts*: The Creator Economy | Remote Work Benefits | Coffee Culture (£5 brew) | Consistency vs. Intensity in Fitness.
  - **Intermediary**: Use a categorical 2-point framework and clear wrap-up.
    - *Prompts*: Artificial Intelligence (Automation vs. Augmentation) | Psychological Safety in Teams | Product-Market Fit | The Danger of Tracking the Wrong Metric.
  - **Professional**: Reframe with an executive thesis and macroeconomic / systemic analogy.
    - *Prompts*: The Attention Economy | De-globalisation & Supply Chains | The Paradox of Choice | Legacy vs. Agility in Tech Startups.

### 4. Explain It Simply\*
- **Problem**: Technical and domain specialists fail to translate concepts for non-expert stakeholders.
- **Workflow**: A technical concept is presented with a designated non-expert listener. User must explain without forbidden jargon.
- **Level Matrix**:
  - **Beginner**: Conversational tone, accessible vocabulary.
    - *Prompt 1*: Explain Inflation to a high-school student wondering why their favorite snack increased in price.
    - *Prompt 2*: Explain your job to a 5-year-old child asking, "What do you actually do all day at your desk?"
  - **Intermediary**: Anchor with an intuitive analogy within 15–20s.
    - *Prompt 1*: Explain Ecommerce to a medieval blacksmith who has magically traveled to the present day.
    - *Prompt 2*: Explain Search Engine Optimization (SEO) to a traditional pastry chef seeking local foot traffic.
    - *Prompt 3*: Explain Data Privacy to a retail shop assistant who wants to understand why websites ask to "Accept All."
  - **Professional**: Land core analogy within 7 seconds; under 12 words per sentence average.
    - *Prompt 1*: Explain Quantum Computing to a busy corporate Sales Director who only cares about speed, revenue, and things that work today.
    - *Prompt 2*: Explain Machine Learning to a historian specializing in ancient civilizations who views modern technology with deep skepticism.

### 5. Storytelling Gym\*
- **Problem**: Data dumps lose audiences; corporate presentations lack memorable narrative structure.
- **Workflow**: User is given a workplace scenario. 60s to tell a structured 3-part story (Setup/Tension, Turning Point, Strategic Takeaway).
- **Level Matrix**:
  - **Beginner**: 3-part timeline (roughly 20s each).
    - *Prompt*: Tell a 60-second story about a time an unexpected morning disaster (e.g., spilled coffee, missed train) derailed your schedule, and how you recovered.
  - **Intermediary**: Clear workplace roadblock and process improvement lesson.
    - *Prompt*: Describe a situation where a skeptical stakeholder resisted your proposal, and how you successfully shifted their perspective.
  - **Professional**: Elevate tension to systemic stakes; end with a profound cultural or strategic metric.
    - *Prompt*: Shifting company culture from hierarchical to agile/autonomous. Deliver a narrative convincing legacy employees to embrace the friction of change.

---

### 6. Connect the Dots\*
- **Problem**: Scripted, linear minds struggle with spontaneous synthesis and cross-domain analogies.
- **Workflow**: Two unrelated concepts flash on screen. 5s countdown, then 60s speech building a logical bridge.
- **Prompts**:
  - `[Gardening in winter]` 🔗 `[Venture Capital (VC) investing]`
  - `[Architectural blueprints]` 🔗 `[Corporate culture transformation]`
  - `[Playing a game of Chess]` 🔗 `[Artificial Intelligence ethics]`
  - `[A severe thunderstorm]` 🔗 `[Microeconomic market disruption]`
  - `[A broken coffee machine]` 🔗 `[Our Q4 sales strategy]`
  - `[A delayed flight]` 🔗 `[Managing client expectations]`
  - `[Assembling flat-pack furniture]` 🔗 `[Onboarding a new employee]`
  - `[An untied shoelace]` 🔗 `[A software code bug]`

---

### 7. Debate Me\*
- **Problem**: Conversational freeze, defensive tone, or rising pitch when confronted with objections.
- **Workflow**: User hears/reads an aggressive objection and must respond with composure and power pauses.
- **Prompts**:
  - *Colleague*: "Why working from the office 5 days a week is better than remote work."
  - *Family Member*: "Why a staycation is better than an expensive holiday abroad."
  - *Strict Project Manager*: "Why your 2-week deadline extension request reveals poor time management."
  - *Assertive Customer*: "Demanding a full subscription refund because the product did not deliver."
  - *Skeptical VC*: "Challenging your unit economics and TAM mid-pitch."
  - *Hostile Journalist*: "Inquiring about blame regarding a corporate data breach."
