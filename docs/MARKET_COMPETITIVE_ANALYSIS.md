## 3. Hakkiri's Strategic Moat
1. **The Daily Gym Paradigm**: 3-minute high-frequency workouts (Daily Anchor) prevent burnout and embed neuromuscular habits.
2. **High-Stakes Corporate Crutch Detection**: Beyond generic "um" and "uh", detecting permission-seeking phrases (`"does that make sense?"`, `"basically"`, `"sort of"`).
3. **Cognitive Synthesis Drills**: Activities like *Cut It In Half* and *Connect the Dots* train lateral thinking and executive brevity that generic speech apps ignore.
4. **Transparent & Fair Scoring**: No accent penalties; pure focus on intelligibility and authority.
